# Representations and Warranties

## Traditional Version

> Each party represents and warrants to the other party that: (a) it is duly organized, validly existing, and in good standing under the laws of the jurisdiction in which it is organized; (b) it has the full corporate power and authority to execute, deliver, and perform this Agreement; (c) the execution, delivery, and performance of this Agreement has been duly authorized by all necessary corporate action; (d) this Agreement constitutes a legal, valid, and binding obligation of such party, enforceable against it in accordance with its terms; and (e) the execution, delivery, and performance of this Agreement does not conflict with any other agreement to which such party is a party or by which it is bound. Provider further represents and warrants that: (i) the Services will be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner consistent with generally accepted industry standards; (ii) the Services will conform in all material respects to the Documentation; and (iii) to Provider's knowledge, the Services do not and will not infringe or misappropriate any third party's intellectual property rights. THE FOREGOING WARRANTIES ARE EXCLUSIVE AND IN LIEU OF ALL OTHER WARRANTIES, WHETHER EXPRESS, IMPLIED, STATUTORY, OR OTHERWISE. PROVIDER SPECIFICALLY DISCLAIMS ANY IMPLIED WARRANTIES OF MERCHANTABILITY, FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE, AND NON-INFRINGEMENT.

## Plain Language Rewrite

> **1. Mutual Representations**
>
> Each party represents that:
>
> 1. It is a validly existing legal entity with the authority to enter into this Agreement.
> 2. Signing this Agreement does not violate any other agreement it is bound by.
> 3. The person signing has the authority to bind the party.
>
> **2. Provider Warranties**
>
> Provider warrants that:
>
> 1. The Service will perform materially as described in the Documentation.
> 2. Provider will provide the Service in a professional manner consistent with industry standards.
> 3. To Provider's knowledge, the Service does not infringe any third party's intellectual property rights.
> 4. The Service will comply with applicable laws in all material respects.
>
> **3. Customer Warranties**
>
> Customer warrants that:
>
> 1. Customer Data will not violate any third party's rights or applicable law.
> 2. Customer has the rights necessary to provide Customer Data to Provider.
>
> **4. Warranty Disclaimer**
>
> Except for the warranties above, Provider provides the Service "as is." Provider does not make any implied warranties, including warranties of merchantability or fitness for a particular purpose.
>
> **5. Warranty Remedy**
>
> If the Service fails to meet a warranty in Section 2:
>
> 1. Customer will notify Provider in writing, describing the non-conformity.
> 2. Provider will use reasonable efforts to correct the issue within 30 days.
> 3. If Provider cannot correct the issue, Customer may terminate this Agreement and receive a pro-rata refund of prepaid fees for the unused portion of the term.

## What Changed and Why

- **Removed corporate-formation boilerplate.** "Duly organized, validly existing, and in good standing" is standard boilerplate that adds length without practical value in most commercial SaaS agreements. "Validly existing legal entity" suffices.
- **Added Customer warranties.** The traditional version is silent on what Customer promises about its data. The rewrite makes Customer warrant that its data doesn't violate third-party rights.
- **Added a warranty remedy.** The traditional version disclaims implied warranties but doesn't specify what remedy exists for breach of the express warranties. Without a remedy, the warranties are toothless.
- **Removed ALL-CAPS disclaimer.** Bold headings and clear language achieve conspicuousness without shouting.
- **Added a law compliance warranty.** Provider should warrant that its service complies with applicable laws — this is commonly negotiated but often missing from initial drafts.
- **Separated representations from warranties.** Representations are statements of fact at signing. Warranties are ongoing promises. The rewrite organizes accordingly.
