# Indemnification

## Traditional Version

> CUSTOMER SHALL INDEMNIFY, DEFEND, AND HOLD HARMLESS PROVIDER AND ITS OFFICERS, DIRECTORS, EMPLOYEES, AGENTS, SUCCESSORS, AND ASSIGNS (COLLECTIVELY, THE "PROVIDER INDEMNIFIED PARTIES") FROM AND AGAINST ANY AND ALL CLAIMS, DEMANDS, ACTIONS, SUITS, PROCEEDINGS, LOSSES, DAMAGES, LIABILITIES, COSTS, AND EXPENSES (INCLUDING REASONABLE ATTORNEYS' FEES AND COURT COSTS) ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO: (A) CUSTOMER'S USE OF THE SERVICES IN VIOLATION OF THIS AGREEMENT; (B) CUSTOMER'S BREACH OF ANY REPRESENTATION OR WARRANTY SET FORTH HEREIN; OR (C) CUSTOMER'S VIOLATION OF ANY APPLICABLE LAW, RULE, OR REGULATION. PROVIDER SHALL PROMPTLY NOTIFY CUSTOMER OF ANY SUCH CLAIM AND SHALL PROVIDE CUSTOMER WITH REASONABLE COOPERATION IN THE DEFENSE THEREOF AT CUSTOMER'S SOLE COST AND EXPENSE.

## Plain Language Rewrite

> **1. Mutual Indemnification**
>
> Each party (the "Indemnifying Party") will defend, indemnify, and hold harmless the other party from third-party claims arising from:
>
> 1. The Indemnifying Party's breach of this Agreement.
> 2. The Indemnifying Party's negligence or willful misconduct.
> 3. The Indemnifying Party's violation of applicable law.
>
> **2. Additional Provider Indemnification**
>
> Provider will also defend Customer from third-party claims that the Service infringes a third party's intellectual property rights.
>
> **3. Additional Customer Indemnification**
>
> Customer will also defend Provider from third-party claims arising from Customer Data or Customer's use of the Service in violation of Provider's acceptable use policy.
>
> **4. Indemnification Process**
>
> The party seeking indemnification will:
>
> 1. Notify the Indemnifying Party within 15 days after learning of the claim.
> 2. Give the Indemnifying Party control of the defense.
> 3. Cooperate reasonably with the defense at the Indemnifying Party's expense.
>
> **5. Settlement**
>
> The Indemnifying Party may not settle a claim in a way that admits fault on behalf of the other party or imposes obligations on the other party without that party's written consent.

## What Changed and Why

- **Made it mutual.** The traditional version only required Customer to indemnify Provider. In most commercial relationships, both parties should share indemnification obligations.
- **Added IP indemnification for Provider.** Customers typically need assurance that the service they're paying for won't expose them to IP infringement claims.
- **Removed ALL-CAPS.** Bold headings provide sufficient conspicuousness.
- **Added process details.** The traditional version vaguely mentioned "reasonable cooperation." The rewrite specifies a 15-day notice period, control of defense, and settlement restrictions.
- **Removed boilerplate party lists.** "Officers, directors, employees, agents, successors, and assigns" is unnecessary — indemnifying the entity covers its personnel.
