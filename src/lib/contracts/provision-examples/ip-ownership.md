# IP Ownership

## Traditional Version

> As between the parties, Provider shall own and retain all right, title, and interest in and to the Service, the Software, and all related technology, documentation, and intellectual property rights therein, including all modifications, improvements, derivative works, and enhancements thereof, whether made by Provider, Customer, or any third party on behalf of either party. Customer shall own all right, title, and interest in and to the Customer Data. Provider hereby acknowledges that it acquires no right, title, or interest in or to any Customer Data by virtue of this Agreement. Subject to Customer's compliance with the terms and conditions of this Agreement, Provider hereby grants to Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable, non-sublicensable, revocable, limited license to access and use the Service during the Term solely for Customer's internal business purposes.

## Plain Language Rewrite

> **1. Provider Owns the Service**
>
> Provider owns the Service, its software, technology, and documentation — including any improvements Provider makes during this Agreement.
>
> **2. Customer Owns Customer Data**
>
> Customer owns all Customer Data. This Agreement does not give Provider any ownership rights in Customer Data.
>
> **3. Customer Feedback**
>
> If Customer provides suggestions, ideas, or feedback about the Service, Provider may use that feedback to improve the Service without obligation to Customer. Customer is not required to provide feedback.
>
> **4. License to Use the Service**
>
> Provider grants Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable license to access and use the Service during this Agreement for Customer's internal business purposes.
>
> **5. License to Customer Data**
>
> Customer grants Provider a limited license to use Customer Data solely to provide the Service to Customer. This license ends when this Agreement ends.
>
> **6. No Other IP Transfer**
>
> Except as stated in this section, neither party transfers any intellectual property rights to the other.

## What Changed and Why

- **Removed "right, title, and interest."** Saying "owns" is sufficient and clearer.
- **Added a feedback clause.** The traditional version is silent on feedback, creating ambiguity about who owns improvement ideas that originate from Customer usage.
- **Added Provider's license to Customer Data.** Providers need a license to Customer Data to provide the service — the traditional version doesn't grant one, creating a legal gap.
- **Removed "revocable" from the license.** License revocability is a material term that deserves deliberate negotiation, not burial in a list of adjectives.
- **Split into six sections.** Each IP concept gets its own heading for easy reference.
