# Termination

## Traditional Version

> This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue for the Initial Term. Thereafter, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive Renewal Terms of equal duration unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal to the other party not less than sixty (60) days prior to the expiration of the then-current term. Either party may terminate this Agreement upon written notice if the other party materially breaches any provision of this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within thirty (30) days after receipt of written notice specifying the nature of the breach. Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other party (i) becomes insolvent, (ii) files a petition in bankruptcy or has such a petition filed against it, (iii) makes an assignment for the benefit of creditors, or (iv) ceases to do business in the ordinary course. Upon any termination or expiration of this Agreement, all rights and licenses granted hereunder shall immediately terminate, and Customer shall immediately cease all use of the Service and return or destroy all Confidential Information of Provider.

## Plain Language Rewrite

> **1. Term**
>
> This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and lasts for the Initial Term stated in the Order Form.
>
> **2. Renewal**
>
> After the Initial Term, this Agreement automatically renews for additional periods of the same length unless either party gives the other written notice at least 60 days before the current term ends.
>
> **3. Termination for Breach**
>
> Either party may terminate this Agreement if the other party materially breaches it and does not cure the breach within 30 days after receiving written notice describing the breach.
>
> **4. Termination for Insolvency**
>
> Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately if the other party becomes insolvent, files for bankruptcy, or ceases normal business operations.
>
> **5. Termination for Convenience**
>
> Either party may terminate this Agreement for any reason by giving the other party 90 days' written notice. If Customer terminates for convenience during the Initial Term, Customer will pay the remaining fees for the rest of the Initial Term.
>
> **6. Effect of Termination**
>
> When this Agreement ends (for any reason):
>
> 1. Customer's license to use the Service ends immediately.
> 2. Provider will make Customer Data available for export for 30 days after termination.
> 3. After the 30-day export period, Provider will delete Customer Data within 60 days.
> 4. Each party will return or destroy the other party's Confidential Information.
>
> **7. Surviving Provisions**
>
> The following sections survive termination: Intellectual Property, Confidentiality, Limitation of Liability, Indemnification, and General Provisions.

## What Changed and Why

- **Added termination for convenience.** The traditional version only allows termination for breach or insolvency. Customers commonly negotiate convenience termination rights.
- **Added a data export period.** The traditional version says "cease all use" immediately but doesn't address what happens to Customer Data. The rewrite gives Customer 30 days to export data.
- **Added data deletion timeline.** Specifying when Provider will delete data after the export window avoids ambiguity.
- **Added survival clause.** Identifying which provisions survive termination prevents arguments about post-termination obligations.
- **Removed "all rights and licenses granted hereunder."** Replaced with specific, actionable consequences.
- **Broke insolvency triggers into plain language.** "Makes an assignment for the benefit of creditors" is legalese; "ceases normal business operations" covers the intent.
