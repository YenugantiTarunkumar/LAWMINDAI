# Data Protection

## Traditional Version

> Provider shall process Personal Data solely on behalf of and in accordance with Controller's documented instructions and shall implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk of processing, taking into account the state of the art, the costs of implementation, and the nature, scope, context, and purposes of processing, as well as the risk of varying likelihood and severity for the rights and freedoms of natural persons. Provider shall ensure that persons authorized to process the Personal Data have committed themselves to confidentiality or are under an appropriate statutory obligation of confidentiality. Provider shall notify Controller without undue delay after becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach. Provider shall make available to Controller all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with the obligations laid down in this Article and allow for and contribute to audits, including inspections, conducted by Controller or another auditor mandated by Controller.

### Plain Language Rewrite

> **1. Processing Scope**
>
> Provider will process Personal Data only as Customer instructs. Customer's processing instructions are documented in Annex 1.
>
> **2. Security**
>
> Provider will maintain technical and organizational security measures appropriate to the sensitivity of the Personal Data. Provider's current security measures are described in Annex 2.
>
> **3. Confidentiality**
>
> Provider will ensure that all personnel who access Personal Data are bound by confidentiality obligations.
>
> **4. Breach Notification**
>
> If a Personal Data Breach occurs, Provider will notify Customer within 72 hours after becoming aware of it. The notification will include:
>
> 1. A description of the breach.
> 2. The types and estimated volume of Personal Data affected.
> 3. The likely consequences for affected individuals.
> 4. Steps Provider is taking to contain and remediate the breach.
>
> **5. Audits**
>
> Provider will:
>
> 1. Provide Customer with a copy of its most recent SOC 2 Type II report (or equivalent) on request.
> 2. Respond to Customer's security questionnaires within 15 business days.
> 3. Permit Customer to conduct one on-site audit per year with 30 days' advance notice.

## What Changed and Why

- **Replaced "without undue delay" with "72 hours."** Vague time standards lead to disputes. A concrete deadline aligns with GDPR's 72-hour regulatory notification window.
- **Specified notification contents.** The traditional version says "notify" without saying what the notification must contain.
- **Added practical audit mechanisms.** Rather than just "allow for and contribute to audits," the rewrite offers SOC 2 reports, questionnaires, and a structured on-site audit right — reflecting how audits actually work.
- **Referenced annexes.** Processing details and security measures belong in detailed annexes, not in the body of the agreement.
- **Removed GDPR Article-tracking language.** The provision should be drafted in plain language; a compliance mapping table can cross-reference GDPR articles separately.
